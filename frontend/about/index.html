A few words from the studio’s creator

Hello, fellow traveler! Whether or not we’ve met in the world beyond this screen, welcome to Qihang Studio.

I’m Qihang—nice to meet you! I’m currently studying data science at the University of Michigan.

I’m drawn to data, visual art, and literature. Data helps me explore the patterns of the world, images open up new ways of seeing, and words give me room to express myself and think.

Qihang Studio is a little corner I’ve made for these interests. Here, I keep a record of my research, creative work, and reflections, while trying to build a bridge between reason and feeling.
